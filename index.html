    Stratigraphic Formation

    Taphonomy

    Gorjanović-Kramberger divided the Hušnjakovo rock-shelter fill into nine levels, with deposits up to about 11 m deep. ESR dating places the sequence at roughly 130,000 years ago (late MIS 5e), and sedimentation rates suggest it built up in under 10,000 years.
